At the live site, connect a Dynamic wallet on the displayed Monad network. Use the verified funding route, choose the UK Practical Guide sample, select a suggested question, review the current price, and sign the escrow transaction. The app confirms the opening transaction before retrieval. A successful answer has versioned citations, a settlement transaction, and a public receipt. If the service fails before settlement, the buyer may refund the still-open escrow after ten minutes. The owner can pause future access from a separate wallet session.

The demo controls access only through DataVault. A content hash does not prove copyright ownership. A receipt records what this service did, not whether an answer is true. Selected private passages are sent to the configured model provider.
